ETAwake Alarm App
-----------------

### Background

Have you ever been late to work or class because of a slow commute? Transit delays and auto traffic plague major cities like NYC and are a major source of headache for commuters. Morning routines tends to be static - wake up, shower, eat breakfast, and leave by 6:30am. However, traffic conditions are dynamic and commuters often under/over allocate time to arrive at their destination.

### Challenge

Optimize wakeup time based on real-time traffic and transit data (e.g. Google Maps API), in conjunction with user's morning routine activities. If traffic conditions worsen, the alarm will dynamically wake up the user earlier to compensate. Conversly, if the commute seams clear, the alarm could allow for some extra sleep!

### Further Ideas

Integrate with school systems, other news sources to check for school closings (e.g. snow day) and shut off alarm completely. No school = sleep in!
