# Game Master

### Background

Normally, big competition will take a lot of time from the host for preparation for arranging the match schedule and publishing the result. We want to save their time and make game-hosting a super easy thing to do.

### Challenge

We want to play a middle role between actuall game and the host, providing a universial interface for different kinds of games from actuall sports to video games. To do it, we are going to build a system that could host competitive games with any possible schedule. It will be intergrated with apps like calendar and may cooperate with other game host site or app. 

We will record, with users' permission, the final result and possible data as much as possible. We may do datamining on what we collect and also, may build APIs for these data.

### First Story

As online games and Esports are developing fast these days, the needs of hosting online competitions, tournaments or championships are growing fast. However, the game hosts always find hosting games online a frustrating task. There is no one single platform nowadays that can enable the host to manage player registration, arrange the games schedules and publish them, show game results and get feedback simultaneously. Hosts have to use mulitple social networking products to accomplish their goals which is sophiscated and prone to error.

Our product, Game Master (Temporary Name) can do all of these for the hosts. The host only needs to register the contest on our website and have necessary settings, then everything will be done automatically. The schedules of contest will be arranged automatically and published on the website. User can see the game results and leaderboard/bracket in real time and send their feedbacks to the hosts.
